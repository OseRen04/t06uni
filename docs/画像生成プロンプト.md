# 画像生成プロンプト：アプリ利用の有無を対比する4コマイラスト

## 用途

`docs/t06中間報告.md` の「解決の方向性とユーザー体験」節に挿入する概念図。時間割・キャンパスマップ・建物内案内をひと続きの体験としてつなぐことで、教室移動の「使う前・使う時・使った後」がどう変わるかを1枚のイラストで示す。

## 構成：2×2グリッド／4コマ

| 位置 | 場面 | 見せたいこと |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 左上 | 従来：使う前 | 紙の時間割を見ても建物への行き方が分からず、建物の外で立ち尽くす学生 |
| 右上 | 従来：使う時 | 建物内（エレベーター前あたり）で講義室が分からず迷い、時計を気にして焦る学生 |
| 左下 | 提案体験：使う前〜使う時 | スマホの時間割アプリで授業を選ぶだけで、地図とルートが表示され、余裕をもって歩く学生 |
| 右下 | 提案体験：使った後 | 講義室入口でスマホに「到着」の確認画面が表示され、安心して入室する学生 |

## 生成プロンプト（英語・そのまま貼り付け可）

画像生成AIは会話の文脈を持たないため、冒頭にサービス説明（CONTEXT）を明記し、プロンプト単体で意図が伝わるようにしている。

```
CONTEXT (for understanding the scene, not to be rendered as text in the image):
This illustration is for a university mobile app called "Campus Class Navigator." The app helps students who get lost trying to find their next classroom between lectures. Without the app, a student only has a paper timetable listing a room number, but has no idea which building to walk to or how to navigate inside it once they arrive, so they wander and often arrive late. With the app, a student taps their class on a digital timetable, and the app shows: (1) a walking route and estimated time to the correct building on a campus map, and (2) step-by-step in-building directions with real photos of landmarks (entrance, elevator, classroom door) so the student can match what they see in real life with what's on screen, ending with an on-screen "arrived" confirmation. The goal of this image is to visually contrast a student's stressful experience WITHOUT the app against a calm, on-time experience WITH the app.

IMAGE TO GENERATE:
A 4-panel illustration in a 2x2 grid, flat vector app-illustration style, soft pastel color palette (light blue #A8D8F0, mint green, coral pink accents), clean rounded shapes, gentle flat shading, no heavy outlines, consistent minimalist character design across all four panels, no text or dialogue bubbles, no logos, no watermark, no readable text of any kind anywhere in the image (including on phone screens — represent UI elements as abstract colored shapes, icons, and map lines only, never actual letters or numbers).

Two distinct university students appear, each with a fixed, consistent appearance across every panel they appear in:
- Student A ("struggling, not using the app"): short messy black hair, beige cardigan over a white shirt, canvas tote bag, holding a folded paper schedule (a plain rectangle with abstract faint lines to suggest text, no real words).
- Student B ("helped, using the app"): shoulder-length brown hair tied back, navy hoodie, backpack, holding a smartphone.

Top-left panel — "Before using the app, outside": Student A stands outside an unfamiliar modern university building entrance, looking down at the paper schedule with a puzzled, worried expression, one hand scratching their head, a small sweat-drop icon above their head. They clearly do not know which building or direction to go. Soft daylight, glass entrance doors, a plain campus signboard in the background (abstract shape, no readable text).

Top-right panel — "While using no app, lost inside the building": Student A is now inside a plain institutional hallway near an elevator, turning their head left and right anxiously, glancing at a wristwatch to show they are running out of time, slightly hunched and rushed posture. Fluorescent-lit corridor, closed doors with small abstract room-number plaques (no readable digits) lining the hallway, conveying they cannot tell which door is the right classroom.

Bottom-left panel — "Using the app, walking to the building with confidence": Student B walks calmly across an outdoor campus path, holding up a smartphone. The phone screen shows a simplified map UI: a blue route line connecting a "current location" dot to a pin marker on a building icon, with a small rounded info card below it (abstract colored bar shapes representing a walking-time estimate, no real text). Relaxed smile, sunny open background with trees and a campus building in the distance.

Bottom-right panel — "Arrived at the classroom on time thanks to the app": Student B stands at a lecture room doorway, smartphone in hand. The phone screen shows a small rounded card with a green checkmark icon and a tiny photo-thumbnail-style rectangle (representing the landmark photo used to confirm arrival), abstract shapes only, no real text. Warm, confident smile, the door is slightly open revealing a bright classroom interior with a few seated classmates, warm welcoming lighting.

Overall composition: thin white gutter lines dividing the four panels into an even 2x2 square grid. Consistent flat lighting direction and consistent line weight/color grading across all panels. The top row (no app) uses slightly cooler, more desaturated tones to convey stress and confusion. The bottom row (with app) uses warmer, brighter tones to convey calm and confidence, visually reinforcing the before/after contrast described in the context above.
```

## 実施時の注意点

- **CONTEXTブロックの狙い**: 画像生成AI自体はサービスの目的を描画しないが、この説明があることでキャラクターの持ち物や表情（紙の時間割 vs スマホ、地図UI、到着確認カード）の解釈が安定する。特にGPT-4o系などプロンプト理解力の高いモデルで効果が出やすい。
- **「no readable text」の明記**: サービス名やUI文言をAIが崩れた文字として描き込むのを防ぐため、UI要素は抽象図形・アイコンとして指定している。日本語文字はほぼ確実に崩れるため、テキストは生成後にPowerPoint/Canva等で別途重ねる。
- **モデル非依存**: Midjourney・DALL·E(GPT-4o)・Stable Diffusion系のいずれでも動作する書き方。Midjourneyの場合は末尾に `--ar 1:1 --v 6` などのパラメータを追記する。
- **配置案**: `docs/t06中間報告.md` の「使う前・使う時・使った後」表の直後、実写の案内写真（正面玄関・エレベーター前・講義室入口）の表の前に挿入する。イラストと実写のトーン差が誤解を招かないよう、「イメージ図」とキャプションを添える。
